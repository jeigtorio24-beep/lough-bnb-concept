Questions

Good to know.

Anything else, just ask: call +353 42 937 3151 or email info@shalomcarlingford.ie.

What's the difference between a B&B room and an apartment?
B&B rooms are in the family-run house, as a single or a sharing room. The three one-bedroomed self-catering apartments are in the grounds, each with its own living area and kitchen upstairs.
What beds are in an apartment?
Each apartment has a double bed, a single bed and a fold-up single bed.
Is linen supplied in the apartments?
Yes: “All mod cons and linen supplied.”
Which balcony gets the sun?
Both, at different times. The front balconies look over Carlingford Lough and the Mournes and catch the morning sun. The rear balconies look over the Cooley Mountains and catch the evening sun.
How far is it to the town centre?
About a 5-minute walk. The Four Seasons Hotel and Ghan House are about 4 minutes' walk away.
How far is Carlingford from Dublin and Belfast?
Shalom is 80 km north of Dublin and 70 km south of Belfast.
What are the rates, and how do I book?
Ask us: call +353 42 937 3151 or email info@shalomcarlingford.ie with your dates, and we'll tell you what's free and the rate. The enquiry helper below writes the email for you.
Can you suggest places to eat and things to do?
Ask us when you get in touch: call or email.
